Research

Research interests

Observational Cosmology

Analysis of the cosmic microwave background, with emphasis on temperature and polarization correlations and data from Planck and ACT; geometric distances from BAO, Type-Ia supernovae and gravitational lensing; perturbation-sensitive observables such as redshift-space distortions and pre-reconstruction full-shape power spectra; and cross-correlations among cosmic shear, galaxy clustering, CMB and galaxies.

Theoretical Cosmology

Development of theoretical, phenomenological and computational models for dark matter and dark energy, including dark-sector interactions, scalar-tensor theories, modified gravity and dynamical dark-energy models.

Neutrinos & Astroparticles

Research at the interface between particle physics and astronomical data, supported by robust computational methods including Boltzmann codes, statistical inference, machine learning and neural networks.

Cosmological Tensions & Anomalies

Integration of theoretical and observational approaches to investigate tensions in cosmological parameters such as H0 and S8, including possible explanations involving physics beyond the standard cosmological model.

Gravitational-Wave Astrophysics

Analysis of data from LIGO, Virgo and KAGRA to test general relativity and investigate black holes and neutron stars, together with simulations and forecasts for future observatories including Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer and LISA.
