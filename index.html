P(Theory | Data)

Understanding the Universe through theory and data

Our group develops theoretical, phenomenological and computational studies in cosmology and gravitation, connecting fundamental models with current and forthcoming astronomical observations.

Observational Cosmology

CMB, BAO, supernovae, lensing and large-scale structure.

Theoretical Cosmology

Dark matter, dark energy, interactions and modified gravity.

Cosmological Tensions

H0, S8 and astrophysical anomalies.

Gravitational Waves

Tests of gravity, compact objects and future detectors.

Research

From fundamental physics to astronomical data

We combine cosmological observations, numerical methods, Boltzmann codes and statistical inference to investigate the physics of the dark Universe.
